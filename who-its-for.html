Who it's for

For organisations responsible for buildings—and the professionals they rely on.

Robson AI develops practical software for the professionals inspecting, evaluating and reporting on buildings, and for the organisations employing or commissioning their expertise.

Better-structured evidence helps professionals explain what they have found, why it matters and what should happen next.

See where Robson AI fits ↓

Three routes to value

The same professional work, viewed from three responsibilities.

01

Organisations commissioning building advice

For property owners, local authorities and public-sector organisations that rely on qualified professionals to assess buildings and provide clear, defensible advice.

What matters: understanding the evidence, priorities and recommended next actions.

02

In-house property and estates teams

For organisations with building surveyors, property professionals or specialist advisers on staff who need structured evidence, consistent review and clearer reporting.

What matters: maintaining context as information moves from inspection into organisational decisions.

03

Surveying practices and consultants

For professional teams delivering inspections, evaluations and reports to clients across varied buildings, estates and specialist commissions.

What matters: working consistently while keeping professional judgement and client communication visible.

Shared outcome

Professional judgement remains central.

  • 1Organisation

    Employs or commissions qualified building expertise.

  • 2Professional

    Inspects, evaluates and decides what the evidence means.

  • 3Robson AI

    Supports structured evidence, review and communication.

  • 4Decision

    The organisation receives clearer information and next actions.
