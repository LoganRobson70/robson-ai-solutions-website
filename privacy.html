Privacy notice

How this site handles enquiries and optional analytics.

This is an email-first contact path. The site does not use a contact form, payment checkout or customer account. Analytics is optional and only runs if a Measurement ID is configured and you accept it.

What we collect

If you email Robson AI Solutions, your message may include your name, contact details, company name, and project context. If you accept optional analytics, the site may also record limited usage data such as page sections viewed, navigation clicks, scroll depth, mailto clicks, and email-copy interactions.

How we use it

Enquiry information is used to understand your request, reply, and decide whether there is a relevant next conversation. If you accept analytics, the site uses that limited data to understand which pages, sections, and contact paths are being used.

Optional analytics

Analytics stays off unless you accept it. When a Google Analytics 4 Measurement ID is configured for this site, analytics requests will only begin after consent. If no measurement ID is configured, your consent choice is stored locally in your browser but no analytics data is sent.

Services used by this site

The website is hosted by Netlify, which may process basic request data such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, timestamps and security logs to deliver and protect the site. The site uses self-hosted fonts, images, scripts and BuildScan demonstration assets. The optional interactive BuildScan model viewer uses locally hosted Three.js files and only loads the public demonstration model when you choose to open it.

Optional analytics is prepared for Google Analytics 4 and the Google tag script, but the current public site keeps the Measurement ID empty. If analytics is later configured and you accept it, the site may send consented page and interaction events to Google Analytics. Your analytics preference is stored in your browser using local storage under robsonai.analytics-consent.

Email links open your own email application. If you send an email, the message is handled by the email providers involved in delivery and by Robson AI Solutions for the purpose of replying to your enquiry.

How long we keep it

Enquiry information is retained only as long as needed to handle the conversation, assess fit, and keep a basic record of relevant business correspondence. Analytics retention is controlled within the analytics platform only when analytics has been enabled and consented to.

Your choices

You can accept or decline optional analytics in the consent banner and reopen those settings at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion of your enquiry data, email hello@robsonai.co.uk.
