Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about the current product.

Is Building Analyst available now?

No. It is the flagship Robson AI product in development. This site shows the workflow and direction without claiming a finished release.

Which platforms are intended?

The current direction is Apple-platform-first where that supports field work. This is a development direction, not a claim of App Store availability.

What may AI assist with?

AI may help structure source-linked summaries and draft wording. It does not independently diagnose a building condition or decide what should be issued.

What requires professional approval?

A qualified professional evaluates the evidence, checks context and uncertainty, and approves, revises or rejects client-facing findings.

How is assessment data handled?

This public site does not upload assessment data into Building Analyst. Product data-handling arrangements remain in development and will be documented before release.

Are Building Analyst and BuildScan integrated?

No. They are separate products today. A connected evidence ecosystem is a future possibility, not a current capability.
