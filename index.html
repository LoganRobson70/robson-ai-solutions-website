Before we talk

A few practical answers.

Can I use Building Analyst now?

Building Analyst is in development and has not been released. The website shows its intended workflow. Contact Wayne to discuss your inspection and reporting needs.

Does AI make the surveying decisions?

No. AI may help organise evidence and prepare draft wording. A qualified professional checks the evidence, makes the judgement and approves anything issued to a client.

What happens when I get in touch?

The button opens a draft in your email app. Tell Wayne about your role, your current workflow and where it slows you down. You can also copy the email address below.

Is the 3D model part of Building Analyst?

The model demonstrates BuildScan, a separate product in development. Integration with Building Analyst is a future possibility.
