Focus

Sharper tools for skilled building work.

Robson AI Solutions exists to turn building knowledge into useful digital capability.

Building work produces evidence everywhere: site notes, photos, observations, measurements, drone captures, client questions and operational decisions.

The opportunity is to make that information easier to capture, structure, analyse and use.

Best fit

For external building capture, visual inspection support and 3D model thinking where photographs alone do not tell the whole story.

Property operations thinking

For estates, FM and building teams trying to make maintenance, compliance, evidence and decision records easier to use.

  • Relevant to WAIS-style estate intelligence and CAFM-adjacent workflows.

BuildScan exploration

Model views make external capture easier to review.

BuildScan is being explored as a local workflow for turning external capture material into inspectable building models. The view below is captured from a GLB loaded through the BuildScan model viewer, showing how site context can be reviewed as a 3D asset.

01 Open GLB

Bring a model output into a focused BuildScan viewer instead of leaving it buried in a processing folder.

02 Review context

Inspect the model as a site asset, with surrounding roads, roofs and external context visible in one place.

03 Prepare evidence

Use the model as a review aid for visual communication, not as a substitute for professional judgement.
