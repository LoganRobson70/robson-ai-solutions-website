About Robson AI

Built from surveying practice, not around AI claims.

Robson AI Solutions is a British product studio founded by Wayne Robson. It starts with evidence, workflow and professional responsibility, then applies software and AI where they can genuinely help.

Products are developed with clear maturity labels. Draft guidance stays subject to qualified review, and future ideas are not presented as working integrations.

The public site uses no account or contact form. Its fonts, scripts and BuildScan demonstration assets are self-hosted; the optional model loads only when requested.
